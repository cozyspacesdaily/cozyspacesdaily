Privacy Policy

Introduction

Welcome to Cozy Spaces Daily. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with our social media presence and content sharing activities, including the use of the Pinterest API.

Last updated: May 2026

Who We Are

Cozy Spaces Daily is a content creator brand focused on sharing home inspiration, décor ideas, and cozy living aesthetics. Our main channels include Pinterest, Instagram, and Linktree (linktr.ee/cozyspacesdaily).

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information directly from visitors or followers. We do not operate an email list, account registration system, or any form that gathers personal data from users.

Any analytics or usage data we access (such as Pinterest analytics or Linktree insights) is aggregated and anonymised — we cannot identify individual users from this data.

Pinterest API Use

Cozy Spaces Daily uses the Pinterest API solely to manage and publish content to our own Pinterest business account. We do not use the API to access, collect, or store data from any other Pinterest users.

Our use of the Pinterest API complies with Pinterest's Developer Guidelines and API Terms of Service.

Third-Party Services

Our content is distributed through the following third-party platforms, each with their own privacy policies:

Pinterest: policy.pinterest.com/en/privacy-policy

Linktree: linktr.ee/s/privacy

Instagram / Meta: privacycenter.instagram.com/policy

Cookies

Cozy Spaces Daily does not operate a standalone website that uses cookies. Any cookies encountered when visiting our Linktree or social media profiles are governed by those platforms' respective cookie policies.

Your Rights (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Union, you have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectify, or erase any personal data we may hold about you.

Since we do not collect personal data directly, there is typically no data to provide or delete. If you have any concerns, please contact us at the address below.

Children's Privacy

Our content is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect any information from minors.

Changes to This Policy
